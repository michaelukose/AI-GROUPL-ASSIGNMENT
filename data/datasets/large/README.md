# Large University Dataset

A comprehensive university-wide examination timetabling benchmark.
- **30 Exams** across 10 academic departments (CS, Math, Physics, Chemistry, Biology, Business, Economics, Law, Psychology)
- **12 Timeslots** spanning 4 days with 3 periods per day (Morning, Afternoon, Evening)
- **8 Exam Rooms** ranging from seminar rooms (30 capacity) to grand auditoriums (200 capacity)
- **70 Students** with realistic degree-track enrollments and cross-disciplinary electives
- High conflict density across shared foundational courses (Calculus, Programming, Principles of Management)
